About the Society

History, mission, and scope of the Zoological & Botanical Society

Society History

The Zoological and Botanical Society of Sikkim Alpine University was established in 2010 by a collective of faculty and students passionate about documenting and conserving the extraordinary biodiversity of the Eastern Himalayas. Beginning as an informal study group, the Society formally launched its peer-reviewed journal in 2012 and has since grown into a respected academic body in the region.

Today the Society serves as a platform for students, researchers, and naturalists across Northeast India and neighbouring countries to share original research, reviews, and field observations.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to foster rigorous scientific inquiry in the life sciences, with a focus on the unique flora and fauna of the Himalayan belt. We are committed to open-access publishing, making research freely available to academics and the public alike.

We envision a community of scientists and citizens working together to understand and protect the biological heritage of the Sikkim Himalaya.

Scope of the Society

  • 🐛
    Entomophagy & Entomotherapy — Research on edible insects and their nutritional profiles, and the therapeutic use of insects and insect-derived compounds in traditional and modern medicine.
  • 🌿
    Plant Studies — Floristic surveys, phytochemistry, plant physiology, and conservation of endemic and endangered plant species of Sikkim.
  • 🧓
    Indigenous Knowledge — Documentation of traditional ecological knowledge held by Lepcha, Bhutia, and Nepali communities regarding medicinal plants and animal use.
  • 🦋
    Invertebrate Zoology — Taxonomy and ecology of insects, spiders, molluscs, and other invertebrates across altitudinal gradients.
  • 🌲
    Conservation Biology — Population viability analyses, habitat mapping, and policy-relevant research for biodiversity conservation.

Open Access Statement

All articles published by the Zoological and Botanical Society journal are freely available online immediately upon publication. We believe that open access to research accelerates scientific progress and benefits society as a whole. Authors retain copyright under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence.

There are no article processing charges (APCs) for authors affiliated with Sikkim Alpine University.

For Authors

Submission Guidelines

Manuscript Format
Article File
Review Process
📄 All manuscripts must be submitted as .docx files. PDF submissions are not accepted for initial review.

Paper Types Accepted

  • Original Research Articles (up to 8,000 words)
  • Review Articles (up to 12,000 words)
  • Short Communications (up to 3,000 words)
  • Field Notes & Observations (up to 1,500 words)
  • New Species Descriptions (as per ICZN / ICN standards)

Formatting Requirements

  • Font: Times New Roman, 12pt, double-spaced
  • Margins: 2.5 cm on all sides
  • Sections: Title · Abstract · Keywords · Introduction · Materials & Methods · Results · Discussion · Conclusion · References
  • Abstract: 200–250 words, structured, no citations
  • Keywords: 5–8 terms, separated by semicolons
  • References: APA 7th Edition style

Figures & Tables

  • Figures: minimum 300 dpi, submitted as separate TIFF or JPG files
  • Figures and tables should be numbered consecutively and cited in text
  • Captions must be self-explanatory
📦 Submit all files together as a single ZIP archive named: AuthorSurname_Year_ZBS.zip

Files to Include

  • Main Manuscript — Author names and affiliations removed (blinded for review) · Filename: manuscript_blinded.docx
  • Title Page — Full title, all author names, affiliations, corresponding author contact · Filename: title_page.docx
  • Figures — Each figure as a separate file · Filenames: Fig1.tif, Fig2.tif etc.
  • Cover Letter — PDF, addressed to the Editor-in-Chief, confirming originality and no duplicate submission
  • Supplementary Data (if any) — Excel or CSV files for datasets

Ethical Requirements

  • Studies involving vertebrates must include ethics committee approval number
  • Field collection of protected species must include relevant permit numbers
  • All authors must declare conflicts of interest (or state "none")
  • AI-assisted writing must be disclosed in the Methods section

Review Timeline

  • Initial screening by Editorial Office: 1–2 weeks
  • Double-blind peer review: 6–10 weeks
  • Decision communicated to authors: within 12 weeks
  • Revised manuscript review: 3–4 weeks
  • Final acceptance to publication: 4–6 weeks

Possible Decisions

  • Accept as submitted
  • Minor revisions required
  • Major revisions required
  • Reject with option to resubmit
  • Reject
The journal follows a strict double-blind peer review process. Author and reviewer identities are kept confidential throughout.
